## How to Use This Curriculum

1. Study each module in sequence.
2. Attempt the exercises before looking at the solutions.
3. Complete the external practice problems linked within each module.
4. Take the concept checks after completing each subject.
5. Practise the timed mock interviews.
6. Revise weak areas before attempting the final assessments.

**Goal:** Build the skills and confidence required to clear fresher-level data and analytics interviews.
